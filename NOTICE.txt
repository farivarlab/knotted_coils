STATUS: exploratory research software. The design tool's electromagnetic outputs (coupling coefficients, B1 maps) are
quasi-static geometric estimates (thin-wire, low-frequency model; no capacitors, tissue or wave effects). The v1.7
full-wave co-simulation (sim/) adds capacitors, a placeholder head phantom and wave effects, but is unvalidated against
measurement. Since v1.9 crossings between coils are separated by outward bumps; in dense knot arrays some
crossings cannot be separated and the app marks those arrays "not buildable as drawn" (open item).
A proposed v1.11 (2026-10-23, pending Reza Farivar's approval) links the app to COMSOL; its results are exploratory.
Confirm any design with full-wave electromagnetic simulation or bench measurement before building.
